Formula One Grand Prix in the browser

MicroProse's 1991 game, unchanged, running in an emulator. A new renderer reads the game's state as it runs and draws the 3D view at your screen's full resolution and width. The handling, the other drivers, the timing and the sound are the original game's.

Play: modernWidescreen, every car in 3D with 3D wheels and helmets, smooth haze. Play: classicDrawn as the original: its framing, its picture cars, its haze. Live mapThe game beside a top-down map of every car.

Playing

  • The game loads in a few seconds (about 4 MB). The original screen is on the left; the new view, on the right, starts when you are in the car.
  • Sound starts on your first key press or click.
  • Before the menus the game asks which language your manual is in, then for a word from it: page 85, paragraph 4, line 1, word 11, which is require. The page answers both for you, and picks keyboard control.
  • You can switch between modern and classic with the menu at the top of the page.

How it works

The game runs in js-dos, DOSBox compiled to WebAssembly. The page reads the cars, the camera and the track from the emulated PC's memory every frame and draws them with WebGL, using the game's own projection, colours and drawing rules, which were decoded from the program. The source and the notes are on GitHub.
